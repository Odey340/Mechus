## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information in connection with the Mechus application (“Mechus,” “we,” “our”), including when Mechus is used with Zoom meetings. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Meeting and session data

- **Meeting links:** When you start a session, you may provide a meeting link (e.g. Zoom). We use this only to join or associate with the meeting; we do not log or store full meeting URLs at a level that is accessible in normal operation (see our data privacy practices below).
- **Transcripts and notes:** Mechus may process meeting audio to produce transcripts and notes. This content is stored locally on the infrastructure where Mechus runs (e.g. your server or our hosted instance), in accordance with our retention and access controls.
- **Voice commands:** Voice input used to control Mechus (e.g. wake word and commands) is processed to provide the service; we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the product unless you have agreed otherwise.

### 2.2 Technical and operational data

- **Logs:** We maintain logs for operation and support. We limit what is logged in production: for example, we do not log full meeting links or full transcript content at standard log levels. See our internal [Data Privacy](../../docs/DATA_PRIVACY.md) practices for details.
- **API usage:** If you use our API, we may log request metadata (e.g. endpoints, timing) for reliability and security; we do not log sensitive payloads at standard levels.

### 2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not sell your meeting content, transcripts, or voice data. We do not use meeting or voice data for advertising or unrelated analytics. Where we use third-party services (e.g. optional STT/TTS or LLM providers), their use of data is governed by their respective privacy terms and, where applicable, our agreements with them.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Provide and operate Mechus (joining meetings, transcription, notes, voice commands).
- Maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use meeting content or voice data for marketing or advertising.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

- **Where data is stored:** Transcripts and notes are stored on the systems where Mechus is deployed (e.g. your own server or our hosting environment). We apply access controls and, where applicable, encryption as described in our security documentation.
- **Retention:** We retain transcripts and notes according to our retention policy and your configuration. You may set or request retention limits; we will honour them where technically feasible.
- **Deletion:** You may request deletion of your data; we will process such requests in accordance with applicable law and our data handling procedures.

---

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

- **Zoom:** When you use Mechus with Zoom, Zoom processes meeting data in accordance with Zoom’s privacy policy and terms. We use Zoom’s APIs and SDKs only as necessary to provide the integration.
- **Other providers:** If you enable optional services (e.g. third-party STT, TTS, or LLMs), those providers may process data under their own policies; we recommend reviewing their privacy and data residency practices.
- **Legal and safety:** We may disclose data where required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security, in line with applicable law.

We do not sell or rent your personal or meeting data.

---

## 6. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect the data we process, including:

- Access controls and authentication (e.g. API keys when the service is exposed).
- Secure configuration (HTTPS/TLS 1.2 or higher for external traffic, secure handling of secrets).
- Logging and monitoring practices that minimise exposure of sensitive data.
- Alignment with our internal security and incident response policies.

Details are described in our security and production documentation.
